Flow is a community and commerce project from O’Riley Foundation, a technology organization developing a broader portfolio of projects. This guide covers Flow; its browser, Android, and iOS experiences are at different stages of development.

Some features are demonstrations

Example content, balances, messages, and verification labels do not represent live services. Feature availability varies by platform and by which services are enabled.

02 / Exploring Flow

Know what each action means.

Profiles and accounts

Browser account and profile tools are available where enabled. Demonstration profiles elsewhere should not be treated as real people or connected accounts.

Listings and drafts

The browser can display listings already published. Creating a listing saves a private draft; it does not make the listing public.

Demonstration content

Sample conversations, businesses, wallet amounts, and verification labels illustrate the experience. They do not establish transactions, real funds, or verified identities.

Private report records

A saved private report is not a message to a support or moderation team. Do not use it to request urgent assistance.

03 / Feature limitations

Services still in development.

The following services are not available:

  • Payments, money transfers, and escrow.
  • Identity verification.
  • Live messaging and push notifications.
  • AI services.
  • Public listing publication and moderation.

A screen showing one of these features does not mean that the service is connected or available to use.

04 / Privacy & safety

Make informed choices.

This guide does not ask you to sign in or submit personal information. It has no analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts.

Before using Flow’s account or data features, read the Flow Privacy Policy for information about the product’s data handling and current limitations.

Keep demonstrations free of sensitive information

Avoid putting identity documents, payment details, passwords, or other sensitive information into demonstration fields or example conversations.
